# 🚀 Deploy to Vercel - RIGHT NOW

## Quick Steps (2 minutes):

1. **Go to:** https://vercel.com/new
   (I've opened this in your browser)

2. **Sign in** with GitHub, GitLab, or Email

3. **Click "Browse"** or drag the entire `BigBouncer` folder

4. **Click "Deploy"** - That's it!

5. **Your site will be live** at a URL like: `https://bigbouncer-xxxxx.vercel.app`

## What Happens:

✅ Vercel automatically detects it's a static site
✅ No configuration needed
✅ Deploys in ~30 seconds
✅ Free SSL certificate included
✅ Global CDN automatically

## After Deployment:

1. **Test your site:**
   - Visit the URL Vercel gives you
   - Try viewing schedules (public access)
   - Click "Admin Login" and enter password: `BigBouncer2025!`

2. **Change Admin Password:**
   - Edit `app.js` line 4: `const ADMIN_PASSWORD = "YourNewPassword";`
   - Re-upload to Vercel

3. **Custom Domain (Optional):**
   - Go to your project in Vercel dashboard
   - Settings → Domains → Add your domain
